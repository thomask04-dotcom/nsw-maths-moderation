Common Grade Scale

Moderation Rationale: Extensive

Extensive
Two-Dimensional Spatial Structure (MA1-2DS-01 & 02)

Extensive: Applies extensive knowledge and skills to new situations by constructing an accurate 4 × 5 tile array without gaps or overlaps, efficiently calculating total area using array skip-counting rather than counting by ones, and autonomously incorporating 6 extra tiles into a balanced composite footprint.

Three-Dimensional Spatial Structure (MA1-3DS-02)

Extensive: Reasons about spatial structure by stacking cubes into distinct layers (6 + 6 = 12). Successfully integrates 12 additional cubes into a stepped symmetrical structure, demonstrating volume conservation and structural balance.

Working Mathematically (MAO-WM-01)

Extensive: Chooses efficient techniques, adapts them to solve unfamiliar problems, and communicates thinking clearly with precise mathematical terminology (array, rows, layers, symmetry, fold line, volume).

Next Pedagogical Steps (Depth, Not Acceleration):

Explore how many different rectangular prisms can be formed from the same 24 cubes (e.g., 2 × 3 × 4, 1 × 4 × 6, and 2 × 2 × 6) to consolidate volume conservation, and introduce fractional partitioning (half-tiles) to measure irregular borders.

Assessment Task Brief (Sequence 10)

Context: Tabletop Museum Exhibition Booth Challenge

Design a tabletop Ooshie Display Booth using square tiles, blocks, and pattern cards.

  1. Booth Floor (Area): Cover surface in rows/columns without gaps or overlaps.
  2. Display Pedestal (Volume): Build solid prisms using uniform cubes and count using layers.
  3. Banner (Transformations): Create a slide or symmetrical design using pattern blocks.

Common Grade Scale & Stage 1 Rubric

Key principle: depth, not acceleration

Extensive achievement reflects extensive knowledge and skills in Stage 1 content, applied to new situations. It does not require content from Stage 2.

Extensive: Extensive knowledge and skills, readily applied, and a very high level of competence in the processes and skills, applied to new situations.
Thorough: Thorough knowledge and skills and a high level of competence in the processes and skills, applied to most situations.
Sound: Sound knowledge and skills in the main areas of content and an adequate level of competence in the processes and skills.
Basic: Basic knowledge and skills and a limited level of competence in the processes and skills.
Elementary: Elementary knowledge and skills in few areas of the content and very limited competence in some of the processes and skills.

Adapted from the NESA Common Grade Scale (Years 1–10). Judgements are made on balance across the whole task.

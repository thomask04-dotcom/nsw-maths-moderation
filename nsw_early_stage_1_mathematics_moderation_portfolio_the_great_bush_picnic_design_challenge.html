2. Repeating Pathway to Picnic (Gum Leaf & Flower AABB)

✓ Clear repeating unit of 4

"My pattern is: Leaf, Leaf, Flower, Flower, Leaf, Leaf, Flower, Flower. The repeating rule is 2 leaves and then 2 flowers. A leaf comes next!"

1 more friend arrived! Now there are 4 friends: Koala, Kangaroo, Wombat, and Echidna.

"I put all 12 strawberry snacks back into the big basket. Then I shared them into 4 bowls: 1, 2, 3, 4."

"Now every friend gets exactly 3 snacks! 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12. There are 0 leftovers, so it is fair!"

Assessment Task Brief: The Great Bush Picnic

Context: The Great Bush Picnic Design Challenge

Design a picnic layout for friends visiting the park with fair shares and a repeating pathway.

  1. Choose Guests: Pick 2, 3, or 4 animal friends coming to the picnic.
  2. Share the Snacks: Take a basket of snack counters (6, 9, or 12). Share them out one by one so every animal gets an equal share.
  3. Build a Pattern Pathway: Create a repeating pathway (AB, ABC, or AABB) leading to the picnic tables.
  4. Draw and Label: Draw your picnic tables, snack groups, and write numerals to label how many snacks are in each group.
  5. Challenge Prompt: One more friend arrives! Can you reshare your snacks equally? Explain what happens.

Protractor Measurement & Construction
MA3-GM-03

Measures and constructs angles up to 360° using protractors and degree symbol (°).

 Precise Measurement: Seamlessly measures and constructs angles in any orientation; self-corrects cumulative drift; tracks baseline zero to eliminate dual-scale errors. Accurate Construction: Accurately measures and constructs familiar acute, obtuse, and reflex angles using protractors; avoids dual-scale errors consistently. Taught Routine Competence: Aligns origin and baseline accurately; selects correct scale using acute/obtuse benchmarks; records with degree symbol (°). Dual-Scale Reversals: Frequently reads incorrect scale (e.g., writing 150° for 30°); requires teacher prompts to verify angle opening before recording. Arbitrary Reading: Cannot place protractor origin on vertex without 1:1 guidance; reads random numbers; omits degree notation entirely.
Reflex Angles & Revolutions
MA3-GM-03

Reflex angles (180°–360°) and angle of revolution (360°).

 Dual Reflex Strategies: Fluently applies subtractive strategies (360° - interior) and validates with circular 360° tools; explains why rays coincide at 360°. Subtractive Fluency: Calculates reflex angles independently by measuring interior angle and subtracting from 360°; labels 360° revolutions accurately. Formula Application: Measures interior angle and subtracts from 360° when following taught anchor chart steps; identifies 360° as a full circle turn. Reflex Misconception: Measures acute interior instead of reflex exterior; requires direct scaffolding to calculate difference from 360°. Not Accessed: Unable to conceptualize turns past 180°; treats angles as isolated line segments rather than rotation.
Angle Classification & Orientation
MA3-GM-03

Acute, right, obtuse, straight, reflex, revolution in varied orientations.

 Mental Rotation Mastery: Classifies and constructs tilted 90° right angles on diagonal baselines; proves perpendicularity is independent of horizontal axes. Independent Classification: Classifies all 6 angle categories accurately in varied orientations; aligns protractor to diagonal rays without turning paper. Standard Orientation Dependence: Classifies standard angles correctly; turns paper physically to align tilted angles with horizontal baselines. Orientation Confusion: Only recognises right angles in standard horizontal/vertical orientation; misclassifies tilted right angles as acute or obtuse. Limited Vocabulary: Confuses acute and obtuse; refers to angles vaguely as "big" or "small" without degree criteria.

NSW Mathematics K–10 Syllabus (2022) Alignment Protocol

Early Stage 1 (Kindergarten) The Common Grade Scale is not required for Early Stage 1 reporting. Samples use its descriptor language for Thorough, Sound and Basic achievement. Oral language captured in teacher scribing serves as primary evidence of mathematical reasoning.
Stages 1 to 3 (Years 1 to 6) Calibrated across all five Common Grade Scale levels: Extensive, Thorough, Sound, Basic and Elementary. Extensive reflects depth and the application of knowledge and skills to new situations, never acceleration to content from higher stages.
Working Mathematically (MAO-WM-01) Embedded throughout: Exploring and connecting concepts, Choosing and applying techniques, and Communicating thinking and reasoning clearly.

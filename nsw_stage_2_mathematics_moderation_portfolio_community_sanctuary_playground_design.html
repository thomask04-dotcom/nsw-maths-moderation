3-Step Navigation Route & Council Cafe Challenge
3-Step Directional Route (Entrance E1 to Native Sanctuary B5):
  1. Step 1: Start at Park Entrance at E1. Walk 2 metres North (forward) along Column E until you reach coordinate E2, with the Picnic Shelter immediately on your right.
  2. Step 2: Turn 90° West (left) and walk 2 metres to coordinate C2, passing just behind the Playground fence on your left.
  3. Step 3: Turn 90° North (right) and walk 3 metres straight ahead through Column C to reach C5, then turn West into the Native Sanctuary entrance at B5.
Council Cafe Challenge Solution & Conservation Proof: "I placed the Cafe in a 1-metre wide boundary along Column J from row 2 up to row 7. The coordinates are: J2, J3, J4, J5, J6, and J7.
Spatial Justification: Even though the space is only 1 metre wide, area is the measure of the total surface space inside the boundary. By extending the building 6 metres in length along the boundary, the array is 1m wide × 6m long = 6 square metres (6 m²). Area is conserved regardless of whether the shape is a squarish block or a long linear strip!"

Teacher Scribing (Verbatim Dialogue)

Teacher: "Maya, why does your 1m × 4m picnic shelter still have an area of 4 square metres if it's not a square?"

"Because area isn't the shape of the outside lines; it's the amount of ground surface inside. A square metre is just a measuring unit. 4 single square metre frames joined in one long row cover the exact same amount of ground as 4 frames put together in a 2 × 2 square."

Grid References
MA2-GM-01

Horizontal before vertical convention.

 High Precision: Autonomous grid setup; explains mathematical rationale for horizontal-first convention; references ranges accurately. Accurate Recording: Carefully coordinates horizontal letters and vertical numbers; no reversals in familiar layout. Taught Routine: Follows horizontal-then-vertical rule correctly in standard tasks with minimal teacher reminders. Inconsistent: Frequently reverses coordinates (e.g. 2B for B2); needs prompts to check horizontal axis first. Arbitrary: Confuses rows and columns; labels letters/numbers randomly; cannot locate cells without 1:1 help.
Area Arrays (m²)
MA2-2DS-03

Rows and columns array structure.

 Fluent Array Reasoning: Multiplies rows and columns (4m × 4m = 16 m²); records numerals and words consistently. Structured Arrays: Uses row-by-column multiplication or skip-counting; records full square metre units accurately. Counting Verification: Applies basic rows and columns but relies on counting squares one-by-one to verify total area. Count-by-One Only: Counts squares individually; omits units or confuses square metres with square centimetres. No Array Structure: Draws blobs across grid boundaries; counts squares inaccurately with gaps and overlaps.
Conservation of Area
MA2-2DS-03

Non-square 4 m² picnic shelter.

 Conceptual Mastery: Explains that area measures internal surface coverage regardless of boundary shape (4 × 1 = 2 × 2 = 4 m²). Accurate Construction: Independently designs a 4m × 1m shelter and articulates why it is not a square. Guided Adjustment: Initially draws a 2 × 2 square; modifies to 1 × 4 when prompted to review the non-square criterion. Misconception: Draws a 2 × 2 square; believes 4 square metres can only be constructed as a square. Unable to Model: Cannot represent 4 m²; does not understand spatial conservation.
Directional Routes
MA2-GM-01

3-step route with turns & landmarks.

 Precise Navigation: Integrates turn angles (90°), cardinal directions (North/East), exact metric distances, and landmarks. Clear Step Sequence: Uses correct left/right turns, distances in metres, and references specific mapped landmarks. Adequate Instructions: Writes a 3-step route with basic turns ("turn left, walk past swings"); landmarks are generic. Vague Directions: Omits distance or specific turn directions ("walk over near the trees"). Gestural / Non-specific: Gives fragmented directions ("over there"); cannot describe route sequentially.
Challenge & Transfer
MAO-WM-01

6 m² cafe in 1m width.

 Novel Transfer: Fluently constructs 1m × 6m cafe; justifies that area is conserved across narrow linear boundaries. Sound Transfer: Correctly maps 1 × 6 cafe and lists coordinates; explains 1 × 6 = 6 m². Partial Transfer: Draws cafe with prompting; struggles to explain how a 6 m² area fits a 1m slot without counting. Persistent Misconception: Claims a 6 m² cafe cannot fit in a 1m space because "6 is bigger than 1". Not Accessed: Requires intensive foundational support on whole numbers and simple grid counting.

The Community Garden Builder (Learning Sequence 6)

NSW Stage 2 Scale: Extensive, Thorough, Sound, Basic & Elementary

Scenario: "You are hired to design a new garden bed layout for our school community! You will partition a 1-metre garden bed into equal fractional sections, allocate crops for Carrots and calculate the complementary fraction for Tomatoes using a bar model, reverse-build a timber herb box from a ⅓ unit fraction, and tackle a nursery extension challenge past 1 whole!"

1 Partition Garden Bed

Fold a 1-metre strip into equal parts (quarters, thirds, or eighths). Mark and label each division.

MA2-PF-01
2 Plant & Label Crops

Shade and label section(s) for Carrots. Identify the denominator as total parts in the whole.

MA2-PF-01
3 Calculate Complement

Draw a labelled bar model showing carrots, tomatoes, and 1 whole. Write the relational equation.

MA2-PF-01 • WM
4 Reverse-Build Herb Box

Given a ⅓ unit fraction strip, iterate 3 times end-to-end to reconstruct the complete 1-whole timber box.

MA2-PF-01 • Reversible
Extension Prompt Extend garden bed to 1½ wholes. Locate and compare ⁵⁄₄ and ¹⁰⁄₈ on a labelled bar model. Explain equivalence beyond 1 whole.

Challenge Prompt Extension: 1½ Wholes, ⁵⁄₄ and ¹⁰⁄₈
Dual Aligned Number Line & Equivalence Model:
Zara's Relational Explanation:
"I noticed that 5/4 and 10/8 are located at the exact same physical position on the garden line! 1 whole is 4/4 (or 8/8). 5/4 is one quarter past 1 whole. Because each quarter is partitioned into 2 eighths, one extra quarter equals two extra eighths (8/8 + 2/8 = 10/8). Therefore, 5/4 = 10/8. They are equivalent fractions greater than 1 whole!"

Teacher Scribing (Verbatim Dialogue)

Teacher: "Zara, how did you verify that 5/4 and 10/8 represent the same length beyond 1?"

"Because 1 whole is 4 quarters or 8 eighths. To get to 5/4, you need 1 more quarter. Since every quarter has 2 eighths inside it, 1 more quarter is exactly 2 more eighths. 8 eighths plus 2 eighths is 10 eighths. It's the same distance!"

Syllabus Focus Extensive Thorough Sound Basic Elementary
Linear Partitioning
MA2-PF-01

Folds strips into equal lengths.

 High Accuracy: Partitions into eighths or thirds with exact creasing; explains halving & non-halving mechanisms. Precise Folding: Partitions into eighths or quarters accurately using repeated halving routines. Taught Routine: Successfully folds quarters or halves with adequate equality using standard folding steps. Unequal Creases: Folds into parts with visible inequality; requires prompts to align paper edges. Arbitrary Folding: Folds or tears strip randomly with no awareness of equal fractional lengths.
Denominators & Families
MA2-PF-01

Identifies denominator as parts in whole.

 Relational Understanding: Reasons about inverse relations (larger denominator = smaller unit length) across families. Clear Conceptual Knowledge: Explains denominator names total equal parts; groups families correctly. Sound Knowledge: States denominator is the bottom number indicating total parts in 1 whole. Inconsistent Naming: Confuses numerator and denominator without teacher reminders. Discrete Numbers: Views numerator and denominator as two unconnected whole counting items.
Complement to 1 Whole
MA2-PF-01

Bar models & relational equations.

 Fluent Part-Whole: Connects complementary parts algebraically (3/8 + 5/8 = 8/8 = 1); explains missing complement. Accurate Bar Model: Draws clear bar model and calculates complement with standard equation. Counting Verification: Uses direct one-by-one counting of remaining units to identify the complement. Guided Correction: Initial miscalculation (e.g. 1/4 + 2/4 = 1); corrects after teacher prompt. Unable to Model: Cannot represent part-part-whole relationships; cannot identify missing fraction.
Reversible Reasoning
MA2-PF-01

Recreates 1 whole from 1/3 unit.

 Autonomous Reversal: Reconstructs whole and explicitly explains why denominator 3 dictates 3 iterations. Accurate Iteration: Iterates 1/3 three times end-to-end to reconstruct full whole length neatly. Sound Execution: Reconstructs whole with minor spacing wobble; recounts 1, 2, 3 pieces to check. Partial Reconstruction: Only traces 2 iterations or leaves large overlapping gaps until prompted. No Iteration: Believes the single 1/3 unit strip itself represents the whole timber box.
Extension & Reasoning
MAO-WM-01

Challenge prompt: 5/4 and 10/8.

 Deep Transfer: Proves 5/4 = 10/8 using linear equivalence past 1 whole with dual bar models. Sound Transfer: Locates 5/4 and 10/8 by counting units past 1; notes they occupy the same point. Limited Extension: Locates 5/4 on number line; struggles with 10/8 across different families. Misconception: States fractions greater than 1 whole are impossible ("can't have 5 quarters"). Not Accessed: Requires intensive foundational support on whole numbers and simple halves.

• Extensive differentiator: Zara applies extensive knowledge and understanding to new situations, demonstrating relational equivalence for fractions greater than 1 (⁵⁄₄ and ¹⁰⁄₈) and explains the multiplicative link between denominators 4 and 8 without prompting.

• Thorough vs Sound differentiator: Marcus (Thorough) applies thorough knowledge and understanding to most situations, reasoning structurally about fractions as measurements of length and explains the complement algebraically (2/8 + 6/8 = 1), whereas Liam (Sound) demonstrates sound knowledge and understanding of the main areas of content but relies on physically recounting individual boxes one-by-one.

• Basic vs Elementary differentiator: Chloe (Basic) demonstrates basic knowledge and understanding, recognising the unit fraction 1/4 after prompting and correcting her miscount, whereas Toby (Elementary) demonstrates elementary knowledge and understanding, treating fraction numbers as disconnected whole items and unable to partition without 1:1 physical assistance.

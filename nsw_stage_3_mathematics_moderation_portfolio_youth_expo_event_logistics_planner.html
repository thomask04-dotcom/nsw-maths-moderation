14,999 - 14,762 = (14,000 - 14,000) + (900 - 700) + (90 - 60) + (9 - 2) = 0 + 200 + 30 + 7 = $237 reserve. This eliminates all trades entirely!"

Phase 2: 2D Area Model & Euclidean Division Proof
Security Hours Area Model (64 × $42):

Sum: 2,400 + 160 + 120 + 8 = $2,688.

Euclidean Capacity Verification:

"Expected attendance is 1,320 youth. Council health guidelines require 1 porto loo for every 75 patrons:
Division: 1,320 ÷ 75 = 17 remainder 45.
Euclidean Proof:
1,320 = (75 × 17) + 45.
Check: 75 × 17 = 1,275; 1,275 + 45 = 1,320.
Reasonableness of Remainder: Because 45 people would be without facilities, we must round the quotient up from 17 to 18 units (18 × $75 = $1,350)."

Council Audit Challenge: Supplier Invoicing Glitch
Elijah's Diagnostic Audit of the $252 Glitch:

"The supplier calculated 6 light rigs at $402 each and recorded $252. A rapid mental estimate proves this is absurd: 6 × 400 = $2,400. $252 is almost 10 times smaller than the magnitude of the real cost!

Diagnosing the Column Error: In the algorithm (402 × 6), the technician computed 6 × 2 = 12 ones (traded 1 ten), then added 6 × 4 hundreds and incorrectly entered it in the tens column without placing the zero in the tens position (402 × 6 = 2,412, not $252$).
Correct Bill: 6 × $402 = (6 × 400) + (6 × 2) = 2,400 + 12 = $2,412.

Surplus Fund Reinvestment:
"Our event plan has $237 contingency reserve. Since 1 porto loo costs $75:
237 ÷ 75 = 3 loos with $12 remaining (3 × 75 = 225; 237 - 225 = $12).
We can safely commission 3 additional sanitation units for the expo!"

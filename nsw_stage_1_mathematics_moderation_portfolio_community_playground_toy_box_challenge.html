2. My Number Sentences

✓ Non-standard relational equality!

10 + (8 + 2) + (5 + 5) = 10 + 10 + 10 = 30

"The equals sign means both sides have the exact same value. 10 + 10 + 10 is balanced with 30."

CHALLENGE PROMPT RESPONSE

30 - 8 = 22 items left!

"I did not count backwards by ones. I partitioned 30 into 20 and 10. I know 10 - 8 = 2 because 8 + 2 = 10. Then 20 + 2 = 22."

Related Fact Family: 22 + 8 = 30 and 30 - 8 = 22.

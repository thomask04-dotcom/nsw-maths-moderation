CHALLENGE PROMPT RESPONSE

30 - 8 = 22 items left!

"I did not count backwards by ones. I partitioned 30 into 20 and 10. I know 10 - 8 = 2 because 8 + 2 = 10. Then 20 + 2 = 22."

Related Fact Family: 22 + 8 = 30 and 30 - 8 = 22.

Moderation Rationale: Extensive

Extensive
Combining & Separating Quantities (MA1-CSQ-01)

Extensive: Applies extensive knowledge and skills to new situations by autonomously reordering multi-addends into tens (8 + 2 = 10 and 5 + 5 = 10) and solving 30 - 8 = 22 using quantity partitioning (30 = 20 + 10) and known bonds rather than recounting.

Working Mathematically (MAO-WM-01)

Extensive: Justifies thinking with precise mathematical language and records non-standard relational equality statements showing deep conceptual understanding.

Depth, Not Acceleration:

Aria demonstrates depth within Stage 1 Part A through flexible partitioning and relational equality, without requiring Stage 2 vertical algorithms.

Where to next

To deepen extensive achievement within Stage 1 (without moving to content from the next stage), Aria could:

  • deepen by solving open problems with many answers (find every pack combination that makes 30) and explaining how she knows she has them all
  • write and explain relational equations with missing numbers (e.g. 10 + __ = 30 − 12)
  • compare her strategy with a classmate’s and explain which is more efficient and why

Assessment Task Brief (Sequence 9)

Scenario: The community playground needs a new toy box. Choose packs from the Park Shop Menu so the box holds exactly 30 items. Show how you worked out the total and write a number sentence.

Park Shop Menu: Jump Rope pack (10) • Skipping Ball pack (8) • Handball pack (5) • Chalk Set pack (5) • Beanbag pack (2)

Challenge prompt: Oh no! 8 items got wet in the rain and must be thrown away. How many items are left? Show a strategy that does not count back by ones if you can.

Common Grade Scale & Stage 1 Rubric

Key principle: depth, not acceleration

Extensive achievement reflects extensive knowledge and skills in Stage 1 content, applied to new situations. It does not require content from Stage 2.

Extensive: Extensive knowledge and skills, readily applied, and a very high level of competence in the processes and skills, applied to new situations.
Thorough: Thorough knowledge and skills and a high level of competence in the processes and skills, applied to most situations.
Sound: Sound knowledge and skills in the main areas of content and an adequate level of competence in the processes and skills.
Basic: Basic knowledge and skills and a limited level of competence in the processes and skills.
Elementary: Elementary knowledge and skills in few areas of the content and very limited competence in some of the processes and skills.

Adapted from the NESA Common Grade Scale (Years 1–10). Judgements are made on balance across the whole task.

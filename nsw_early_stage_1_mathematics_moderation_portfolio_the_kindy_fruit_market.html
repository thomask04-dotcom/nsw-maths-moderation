Assessment Task Brief: The Kindy Fruit Market

Context

Our class is running a fruit market for the school community. You are the stall keeper! Count your fruit, pack bags of 10 and serve your customers.

  1. Count the fruit: How many are in your basket (13–18 pieces)? Show how you counted and write the number on a price tag.
  2. Pack bags of 10: Fill a bag with exactly 10 pieces using two kinds of fruit. How many different bags can you make? Draw and label them.
  3. Serve a customer: There are 8 apples. A customer buys 3. How many are left? (Or: 2 more oranges arrive. How many now?)
  4. Challenge prompt: The banana basket has 12 and the pear basket has 9. Which has more? How many more? Show how you know.

Evidence: work samples, photos of concrete arrangements and teacher scribing of each child's oral explanation. Materials: real or plastic fruit, counters, paper bags, numeral cards 0–20, dot cards.
